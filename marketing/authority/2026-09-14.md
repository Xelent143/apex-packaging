## Guest post ideas

1. **Seven packaging-spec fields growing brands should lock before a repeat order** — dimensions, material, closure, print file, barcode zone, packout, and approval owner; tailored to Packaging Strategies' practical, non-biased format.
2. **Packaging specification drift is a manufacturing-control problem** — show how uncontrolled substitutions, artwork versions, pallet assumptions, and site-level exceptions create operational variation; reserve original expert language for IndustryWeek.
3. **Start protective-packaging selection with the failure mode, not the material** — map movement, shock, abrasion, moisture, corrosion, and presentation risks to test questions without making unverified performance claims.
4. **A buyer's pre-PACK EXPO packaging-spec worksheet** — a neutral checklist for teams meeting material and equipment suppliers; do not imply Apex exhibits at or sponsors the show.
5. **How multi-site shippers can stop packaging SKU drift** — define approved standards, exception ownership, reorder data, and change control for parcel and LTL operations.
6. **What natural-product brands should decide before choosing kraft packaging** — separate substrate appearance, print limits, barrier needs, inserts, and end-of-life claims.

## PR/news angle draft

Status: expert-commentary pitch only; do not publish as an Apex press release without first-party evidence.

**Working headline:** `Packaging buyers need a controlled specification before they need another material sample`

As the North American packaging calendar moves from PMMI's Montreal Annual Meeting to Corrugated Week and PACK EXPO International, Apex Packaging Solutions can offer a useful buyer-side point of view: supplier comparison is unreliable when dimensions are the only controlled input. A short expert commentary can outline the fields buyers should bring to a supplier conversation—product sensitivity, failure mode, material and closure baseline, print/version status, volume bands, route and pallet assumptions, and approval ownership. The angle is timely and educational, but it is not company news and must not claim Apex attendance, event affiliation, quantified outcomes, or customer results without evidence.

Before pitching, a named Apex subject-matter expert should add:

- Three recurring specification gaps observed firsthand, stated without customer identification.
- One anonymized before/after workflow example whose facts can be substantiated.
- Clear boundaries around testing, compliance, sustainability, and lead-time claims.
- Original wording sufficient to meet each publication's human-authorship rules.
