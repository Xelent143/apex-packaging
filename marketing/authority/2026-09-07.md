## Guest post ideas

1. **The packaging specifications buyers should standardize before peak-season reorders** — focus on dimensions, material grades, closures, labels, approved artwork, and reorder ownership. Link to the shipping-supplies standardization guide.
2. **What first-time PACK EXPO buyers should document before comparing suppliers** — a neutral pre-show checklist for volumes, product sensitivity, packout, route, pallet pattern, and approval status. Link to the RFQ template and design-support page.
3. **Why insert selection should start with the failure mode** — compare corrugated partitions, foam, molded pulp, and paper systems by movement, abrasion, shock, presentation, and recovery needs. Link to the September 7 insert guide.
4. **How packaging version control protects regulated product launches** — cover dielines, copy approvals, barcodes, label zones, and revision ownership without making compliance claims. Link to the pharmaceutical packaging guide.
5. **When metal-parts packaging needs barrier protection, not just a stronger box** — explain corrosion exposure, VCI, cushioning, bag closure, and transit variables. Link to the VCI guide.
6. **How multi-location teams turn packaging from a rush purchase into a controlled specification** — connect standard SKUs and exception rules to purchasing resilience. Link to the Canada multi-location buyer guide.
