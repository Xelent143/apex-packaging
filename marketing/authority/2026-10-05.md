## PR/news angle draft — proposed expert feature

**Working headline:** Before changing an insert, document the whole packout

Canadian and US brands comparing protective-packaging proposals need a shared description of the product and how it is packed. A proposed Apex Packaging Solutions expert feature would follow one product through two candidate packouts, recording the carton, insert geometry, loading sequence and handling assumptions before discussing any performance comparison.

The proposed story would distinguish observations about fit and packing from results of a defined evaluation. Original photographs and a short comparison sheet would let readers see which variables were held constant and which changed. No damage reduction, shipping savings or environmental benefit would be claimed without evidence.

Apex's current website describes a London, Ontario-based supplier coordinating custom packaging projects and overseas production for Canadian and US customers. A named company expert could explain how a buyer and supplier agree the brief and document sample decisions. This is a proposed interview and demonstration; neither has been completed for this pack.

**Evidence needed:** named expert and role; permission-cleared product/sample; two documented configurations; dated original photographs; actual observations; expert-approved explanation of any evaluation method and limitations. Use a clearly labelled hypothetical example if no real case is available. A future company-news release requires a separate verified milestone or completed original finding. No invented quotes, customer relationships or study results.
