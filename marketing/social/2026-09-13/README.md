## LinkedIn Post

Kraft is not a neutral background. It is part of the print system.

For a natural product brand, approve the packaging in this order:

1. Define the product, sales channel, and shipping route.
2. Select the box structure, board grade, and kraft tone around real handling needs.
3. Test ink contrast, barcode zones, coatings, and finishes on the chosen board.
4. Review product fit, closure, packing work, and the retail-facing panel on a representative sample.
5. Record the approved board, dieline, artwork version, print method, finish, and packing notes for reorders.

When comparing quotes, make sure every supplier is pricing the same dimensions, material, print scope, insert, tooling, sample path, freight assumptions, quantity, and timing. Unit price only means something when the scope matches.
