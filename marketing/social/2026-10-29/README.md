## LinkedIn post

A box quote is only comparable when the packing scope matches.

For your next ecommerce order, put these details in the brief: product dimensions and weight, fragile or scuff-sensitive areas, shipping route, and the full packing setup.

Then compare the box, insert or void fill, print coverage, sampling and freight assumptions together. If one quote leaves out the internal packaging, its unit price tells only part of the story.

Use a sample review to check how the actual product is packed and removed. Keep the approved specification and packing instructions for reorders.

Apex Packaging’s new ecommerce guide walks through the details to send before production pricing.
