## LinkedIn post

5 details belong beside your approved carton sample: dimensions, board grade, print method, finish and packing instructions.

When the next order is due, those notes give procurement and the packing team a shared reference.

Before asking for repeat pricing:
- Confirm the product and shipping route are unchanged.
- Check the current artwork and label locations.
- State the quantity and delivery destination.
- Ask suppliers to identify changes to materials, tooling, sampling or freight assumptions.

If the product or route has changed, ask whether the sample and specification need another review.
