## LinkedIn

6 details make a soap-packaging quote easier to compare:

• Bar dimensions and weight
• Carton, sleeve or window concept
• Artwork and label zones
• Quantity by size or variant
• Delivery location and handling route
• Sample needs and material requirements

Then review the packed sample: fit, closure, print placement and the areas where the soap contacts the packaging. If moisture exposure is part of the handling route, state the conditions and ask how the proposed material will be evaluated.
