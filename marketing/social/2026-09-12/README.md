## LinkedIn Post

A subscription mailer should be specified for shipment before it is designed for the unboxing moment.

Give the packaging supplier one clear record of:

1. Product dimensions, weight, photos, and scuff-sensitive or fragile areas.
2. Parcel route, handling conditions, delivery destination, and packing method.
3. Mailer construction, board choice, insert, closure, and required internal clearance.
4. Dieline version, colour references, barcode zones, finishes, and inside print.
5. Quantity, launch date, reorder frequency, and any planned SKU changes.
6. Sample requirements and the acceptance notes behind the approved version.

That specification makes quotes easier to compare. Check that each supplier has included the same size, material, print coverage, insert, tooling, samples, freight assumptions, and timing—not only the unit price.
