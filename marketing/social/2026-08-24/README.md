## LinkedIn Post

Premium gift box programs usually go sideways when the quote starts with a rough outer size and leaves the rest of the bundle undefined.

That forces the packaging supplier to guess about product weight, insert fit, wrap style, scuff risk, shipping method, and whether the pack needs to work for retail presentation, parcel delivery, or both.

For a stronger gift-box RFQ, it helps to answer:

1. What products are inside the bundle, and what are their exact dimensions, weights, and fragility risks?
2. Does the pack need a rigid setup box, drawer box, magnetic closure, sleeve, or another premium format?
3. Are inserts needed for spacing, protection, or presentation, and what material direction is preferred?
4. Will the box ship parcel, courier, LTL, pallet, or move through a retail distribution chain?
5. Is artwork ready, or does the team still need dieline and packaging design support?
6. Does the launch need a physical sample to confirm fit, closure behavior, wrap finish, and unboxing flow before production?

That matters because a lower first quote does not help if the insert fit changes later, the board scuffs in transit, or the product bundle needs extra protection after approval.
