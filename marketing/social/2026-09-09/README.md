## LinkedIn Post

A moving or storage box is part of an operating system—not just an empty carton.

For warehouse transfers, office relocations, archive programs, or multi-site moves, align the quote around:

1. Inside dimensions, packed weight, and the contents being moved.
2. Manual lifts, carts, conveyors, pallets, freight, and storage dwell time.
3. Board grade and structure matched to the real stack height and load.
4. Hand-hole size and position tested with a packed sample.
5. Closure and opening steps that suit the packing workflow.
6. Clear label zones that remain visible and scannable after stacking.
7. Quantity, delivery sequence, reorder timing, and the approved spec.

The lowest unit price can become expensive when boxes bulge, handles tear, labels disappear in a stack, or crews need extra time to repack.

Define the route first. Then compare quotes using the same structure, board, print, tooling, samples, freight assumptions, and lead time.
