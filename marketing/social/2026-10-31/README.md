## LinkedIn post

Metal parts bring two questions to a packaging review: what can contact the surface, and what happens along the shipping route?

Before requesting VCI packaging pricing, send Apex the part dimensions, weight, metal type and surface finish. Include your current packing method, expected storage conditions and destination.

Review the complete pack: the proposed paper or film, closure, cushioning and outer carton. Ask which material is appropriate for the actual part and what validation the application requires. A visual sample review alone does not establish corrosion protection.

For repeat orders, retain the approved material specification and packing instructions alongside the sample.
