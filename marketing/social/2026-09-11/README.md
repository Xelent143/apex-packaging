## LinkedIn Post

Multi-location packaging gets expensive when each site is working from a different version of “approved.”

A stronger custom-packaging program starts with one controlled record for:

1. Product dimensions, weight, photos, and sensitive surfaces.
2. Package structure, material, insert, closure, and packing method.
3. Dieline version, colour references, barcode zones, and finishes.
4. The approved physical sample and acceptance notes.
5. Quantity by location, delivery windows, route, and freight assumptions.
6. Revision ownership, reorder frequency, and inventory triggers.

That master specification gives procurement, operations, creative teams, suppliers, and receiving sites the same benchmark. It also makes quote comparisons more useful: the same material, dimensions, print scope, tooling, sampling, freight, and lead-time assumptions.
