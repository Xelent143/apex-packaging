## LinkedIn Post

CBD packaging problems usually start before production, not on press.

The early failure point is often the quote request.

If the buyer asks for a CBD box without clarifying the product format, the route, the panel requirements, and the finish direction, the project slows down in proofs, samples, and approval rounds.

For CBD packaging boxes, a stronger RFQ usually answers:

1. What product is going inside: bottle, jar, pouch, balm, multi-pack, or gift set?
2. Is the package for retail shelf, ecommerce shipping, wholesale case packs, or a launch kit?
3. What material direction fits the goal: SBS paperboard, kraft, rigid board, corrugated, or a mixed-format kit?
4. Which panels need reserved space for client-supplied warnings, ingredients, barcodes, batch codes, and claims?
5. What finish direction matters: matte, soft-touch, foil, embossing, spot UV, or a simpler uncoated look?
6. Is sample approval required for fit, print, closure, or presentation before production?
7. What quantity, launch date, reorder cadence, and delivery destination should the quote assume?

That matters because the lowest first quote rarely stays low if the package scuffs, the closure is awkward, the panel space is too tight, or the route was never defined.
