## LinkedIn post

6 details make heavy-order packaging quotes easier to compare: dimensions, board specification, protective components, print coverage, sampling and freight assumptions.

Start with the actual product weight and handling route. A parcel shipment and a pallet delivery need different details in the brief.

Ask each supplier to quote the same packing configuration. Identify whether inserts, tooling and samples are included, and review loading, closure and label placement with the actual product before approval. Agree on any shipment testing required for your route.
