## LinkedIn post

3 references belong in a retail packaging brief: the actual product, the artwork revision and a physical finish sample.

A render can communicate a concept. Production approval needs more detail:

- Identify the material and finish you want quoted.
- Mark colour references, foil areas and label positions on the artwork.
- Review the packed product, closure and any outer shipping carton together.
- Confirm that quotes include the same print coverage, tooling, sampling and freight assumptions.

For Sacramento home-goods orders, also separate the cost of delivering empty packaging to your packing site from the requirements for shipping finished customer parcels.

Apex’s new luxury packaging material guide helps buyers prepare that brief. Send your dimensions, quantity, artwork and handling route for review.
