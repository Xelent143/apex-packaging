## LinkedIn post

2 parts or 20? The insert brief changes with the packed configuration.

For finished metal components, start with the contact points: painted faces, projecting threads and exposed edges. Then review how the operator places each part, closes the carton and removes the contents.

A sample review should cover:
- Actual part dimensions and individual and packed weights.
- Separation, movement, edge clearance and removal access.
- The outer carton, closure and intended handling route.
- Any shipment testing required before production approval.

A corrugated divider separates components; it does not establish cushioning, puncture resistance or corrosion protection on its own.

Apex’s new custom box inserts guide explains what to include before requesting pricing. Hamilton buyers can also send separate service-order and production-carton configurations for review.
