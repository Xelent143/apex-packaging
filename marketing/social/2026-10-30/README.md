## LinkedIn post

A pallet shipment needs more than a box specification.

For plant teams reviewing industrial packaging, put the handling process in the brief: product weight, container dimensions, pallet pattern, carrier method and delivery destination.

Then add the consumables your operation uses: liner, stretch film, strapping and edge protection. Tell Apex whether wrapping is manual or machine-based, and share the current specification where available.

A sample review can help identify packing or label-placement issues. Agree on the appropriate trial for the actual shipment before treating a material change as ready for rollout.

Ordering on a release schedule? Include monthly volume and reorder frequency in the same brief.
