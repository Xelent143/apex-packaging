## LinkedIn Post

A pallet load is a packaging system—not a stack of boxes with film added at the end.

For LTL and warehouse routes, align the specification around:

1. Product, carton, and pallet dimensions plus total packed weight.
2. Carton strength and a pallet pattern that avoids unsupported overhang.
3. Load height, weight distribution, and any sharp or unstable edges.
4. Stretch-film type and application method suited to the real equipment and route.
5. Corner boards, top sheets, slip sheets, or strapping where the hazard calls for them.
6. Forklift touches, transfers, dwell time, climate, and final-mile handling.
7. A representative trial load and a controlled reorder specification.

Unit price alone cannot show whether a load will shift, crush, snag, collect dust, or require rework at the dock. Compare quotes using the same materials, dimensions, application assumptions, tooling, trials, freight, and lead time.
