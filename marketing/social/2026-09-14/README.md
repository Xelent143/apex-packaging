## LinkedIn Post

“Heavy duty” is not a complete corrugated-box specification.

For a heavy ecommerce order, define the packaging system in this order:

1. Record the product dimensions, total weight, weight distribution, fragile surfaces, and acceptable orientation.
2. Map the route: parcel, courier, LTL, pallet, warehouse handling, and expected stacking.
3. Compare board construction, ECT, flute, internal fit, partitions or corner blocks, and closure as one system.
4. Pack the real product in a representative sample and review movement, clearance, corners, seams, labels, and the unpacking experience.
5. Keep the approved specification, dieline, artwork, packing method, and sample as the reorder benchmark.

When comparing quotes, make sure every supplier is pricing the same size, board construction, print coverage, insert, tooling, samples, freight assumptions, quantity, and timing. A unit price is only comparable when the scope matches.
