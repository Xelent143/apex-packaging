## LinkedIn post

6 pouches in a case is a pack count. It is not a complete shipping specification.

For a dry-goods multipack, include the filled product dimensions, case weight, packing layout and route. A parcel shipment and a pallet delivery may need different packaging decisions.

Before production approval, pack the actual sealed products into a sample. Check closure, label space and the packing sequence. Record the approved board, dimensions, print and packing instructions for the next order.
