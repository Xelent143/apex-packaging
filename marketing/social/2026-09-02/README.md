## LinkedIn Post

Custom product boxes are not just a print decision.

They are a coordination decision between structure, artwork, protection, lead time, and reorder control.

For launch kits and repeat programs, the quote usually improves when buyers define:

1. The product dimensions, weight, and any fragile or scuff-sensitive areas.
2. The sales channel and route, whether that means retail, parcel, subscription, courier, or pallet distribution.
3. The box format, including corrugated shipper, printed carton, mailer, insert, or a mixed pack.
4. The artwork state, including dielines, colour expectations, barcodes, and finish notes.
5. The quantity and launch timing, because MOQ and print method change quickly when volume shifts.
6. The sample requirement, especially when fit, closure, or presentation still needs proof.
7. The reorder plan, so the approved structure and print spec can be repeated instead of rebuilt.

That is the difference between collecting a fast unit price and approving a product-box program that will still work on the next release.
