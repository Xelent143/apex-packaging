## LinkedIn Post

VCI packaging is rarely just a bag decision.

For metal parts and hardware teams, it is a corrosion-control system decision.

VCI film, VCI paper, desiccants, foam protection, and the outer carton need to match the actual product condition and shipping environment, or the lowest quote becomes the most expensive failure later.

Before comparing suppliers, buyers should define:

1. The part type, finish, and any polished, machined, or coated surfaces.
2. The route, including warehouse dwell time, export exposure, pallet stacking, and final delivery environment.
3. The packaging format, whether that means VCI bags, wraps, paper, corrugated kits, or a mixed protective-packaging stack.
4. The sealing path, because open packs and inconsistent closures weaken vapor protection.
5. The moisture-control requirement, including desiccant, liner, or humidity concerns.
6. The sample need, especially when the program is new or the part geometry is awkward.
7. The reorder control plan, so the approved rust-prevention spec can be repeated across shipments.

That is the difference between ordering rust-prevention materials and approving a VCI packaging program that is actually stable in production.
