## LinkedIn Post

Custom mailer box quotes usually break down when subscription brands compare price before they compare assumptions.

The misses often show up here:

1. The buyer sends dimensions but not packed weight, fragility, or scuff risk.
2. Inserts, tissue, cards, or labels are planned later even though they affect fit and pack-out speed.
3. Artwork is still moving, but the quote quietly assumes production-ready files.
4. The first run is a launch, yet samples were skipped.
5. Quantity is shared without reorder timing, so MOQ and production logic get guessed.
6. Parcel handling is expected, but the route was never defined clearly.

For a cleaner custom-mailer RFQ, subscription teams should usually send:

1. Product dimensions, packed weight, and product photos.
2. Whether the package is parcel, courier, pallet, or retail-distribution bound.
3. The mailer style target, or approval for Apex to recommend one.
4. Insert, tissue, card, label, or presentation requirements.
5. Artwork status, print coverage, barcode zones, and finish notes.
6. Quantity, launch date, reorder frequency, and sample expectations.

That gives procurement, operations, and brand teams a better way to compare protection, presentation, lead time, and repeatability before production starts.
