## LinkedIn post

3 checks belong in a kraft packaging sample review: the printed surface, the closure and the shipping setup.

For natural-product brands, the same carton may need to look right on a shelf and fit inside a parcel shipper. Give both jobs a place in the brief.

Review artwork on the proposed board. Pack the actual product and any protective components. Check label space, closure and the steps your team will repeat. Agree on any route-specific shipment testing before approval.

Keep the approved sample with the board specification, dimensions, print method and packing instructions for reorders.
