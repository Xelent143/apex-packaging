## 1 — Carton label review (generated)

Photorealistic professional packaging editorial photograph, portrait 1024 × 1536. Warm neutral studio table, soft directional lighting. Three fully visible folding paperboard cartons: ivory closed front-facing carton, black closed carton turned to reveal a large blank side label panel, and open kraft carton showing an empty interior and realistic folded flaps. Print exact “APEX PACKAGING” wordmark neatly on ivory and black fronts in muted gold. Generous margins around every carton; upper quarter clean negative space. Small plain material swatch beside the cartons. Tactile paper fibers and plausible folds. No additional text, certification symbols, contents, health claims or watermark. Output: `carton-label-review.png`.
