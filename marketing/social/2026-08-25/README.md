## LinkedIn Post

Wholesale packaging quotes usually get slow when the spec is still incomplete.

The buyer may have a target box style in mind, but the real decision still depends on product dimensions, weight, route, artwork state, MOQ, reorder timing, and whether the program covers one SKU or several.

That is where packaging costs and approval loops start to move.

For a cleaner wholesale-packaging RFQ, it helps to define:

1. The exact product dimensions, weights, and any fragile or scuff-sensitive areas.
2. The route: parcel, courier, pallet, LTL, retail distribution, or a mix.
3. The material and format direction, including corrugated grade, folding carton structure, inserts, or protective needs.
4. The artwork state, barcode zones, print expectations, and finish requirements.
5. The quantity plan: initial order, reorder frequency, and any multi-SKU requirements.
6. Whether samples are needed to confirm fit, closure behavior, presentation, or handling before production.

That matters because a lower first quote is not a win if the board grade changes later, the insert gets added after approval, or the reorder plan was not aligned with actual inventory timing.

The latest Apex guide walks through how buyers can compare wholesale packaging quotes more fairly and build a more repeatable reorder process.
