## LinkedIn post

3 specifications belong in a pallet packaging brief: the product unit, the packed case and the pallet load.

Start with dimensions and weight. Add units per case, the pallet pattern and the handling route. Identify material preferences and sample needs before asking suppliers to price the same job.

Then compare what each quote includes: material, size, print coverage, tooling, sampling and freight assumptions. A unit price alone does not describe the scope.

For Moncton retail replenishment, specify the single parcel order separately from the multi-unit case. Both need a fit review around the actual contents.
