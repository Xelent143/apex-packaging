For procurement teams comparing protective packaging, start with the product dimensions, weight, delicate surfaces and handling route. Those details give the carton and insert discussion a concrete starting point.

Ask for a sample when the kit is fragile, the insert fit is close or the pack is new. Review how each item loads, whether the closure works as intended and how the packing instructions will be repeated by your team.

For reorders, keep the approved sample and record the carton dimensions, insert material, print details and packing sequence.
