## LinkedIn post

A cosmetic retail carton and its shipping pack need one coordinated review.

Before a launch, pack the actual product into the retail carton, then review that carton inside the proposed mailer and insert. Check closure, insertion and removal, visible artwork, and surfaces that could rub during handling.

Give the supplier the route as well as the presentation brief: parcel, courier or retail distribution. Include colour references, finish notes, quantities and the launch date so the sample and quote address the same scope.

Apex Packaging’s latest guide explains what to confirm before approving cosmetic packaging boxes. Keep the approved sample and specification as the reference for reorders.
