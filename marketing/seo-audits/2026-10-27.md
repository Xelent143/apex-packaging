## Fix applied

The seven daily posts published from October 21 through October 27 shared a repeated, overlong title/description suffix. This made their snippets less specific and pushed both fields beyond practical search-result lengths.

Shortened each post's source title and description while retaining its topic and shipping/damage intent. The change reduces the generated overlong-description count from 148 to 141; 126 older titles and 141 older descriptions still exceed the 60/160-character guidelines. That remaining backlog needs topic-aware editorial revision rather than a bulk rewrite.
