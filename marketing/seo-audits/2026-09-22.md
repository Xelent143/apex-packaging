## Issues and disposition

### P1 — Daily publishing creates duplicate articles

`scripts/generate-daily-blog.mjs` chooses one of 30 fixed topics with `topics[dayNumber % topics.length]`, then renders the same title, description, FAQs, headings, and article body under a new date-suffixed route. Each blog published this week repeats its corresponding July or August source after normalizing dates and route names:

| New route | Existing copies |
| --- | --- |
| `/blog/protective-packaging-for-fragile-product-kits-2026-09-16` | 2026-07-18, 2026-08-17 |
| `/blog/retail-display-boxes-for-counter-and-shelf-programs-2026-09-17` | 2026-07-19, 2026-08-18 |
| `/blog/cbd-packaging-boxes-for-compliance-and-shelf-impact-2026-09-18` | 2026-07-20, 2026-08-19 |
| `/blog/food-packaging-boxes-for-dry-goods-and-multipacks-2026-09-19` | 2026-07-21, 2026-08-20 |
| `/blog/cosmetic-packaging-boxes-for-retail-launches-2026-09-20` | 2026-07-22, 2026-08-21 |
| `/blog/soap-boxes-for-handmade-and-retail-brands-2026-09-21` | 2026-07-23, 2026-08-22 |
| `/blog/tea-packaging-boxes-for-cartons-tins-and-pouches-2026-09-22` | 2026-07-24, 2026-08-23 |

**Recommended fix:** pause date-only republication, use Search Console performance/indexing data to choose one retained URL per topic, redirect weaker copies to the retained article, remove redirected URLs from the blog index/sitemap, and require a substantively new angle before future publication. This was not applied because deleting or redirecting published URLs without traffic and indexing evidence is not a safe automated change.

### P2 — Blog search snippets are unusually long

The seven new blog titles are 87–109 characters and their descriptions are 170–194 characters. Across the generated site, 91 titles exceed 60 characters and 117 descriptions exceed 160 characters. These are editorial heuristics rather than validity errors, but search results will commonly truncate them.

**Recommended fix:** after duplicate consolidation, add separate concise SEO title and description fields to the generator while retaining descriptive on-page headings. Fixing metadata before consolidation would only disguise, not resolve, the duplicate-article problem, so no automated edit was made.
