## Fixes applied

### P2 — Shared schema property/type mismatch

In `src/lib/schema.ts`, replaced `makesOffer: OfferCatalog` with `hasOfferCatalog: OfferCatalog`. The catalog describes custom packaging services; its offered entries now use `Service`, compatible with their `provider` and `areaServed` properties. This changes structured data only.

Schema.org defines [hasOfferCatalog](https://schema.org/hasOfferCatalog) for an organization's catalog, while [makesOffer](https://schema.org/makesOffer) expects an Offer. [provider](https://schema.org/provider) describes the service provider relationship. Parsed rebuilt organization JSON-LD and verified the corrected catalog and Service entries across every generated page.

### P3 — Two internal links still used consolidated URLs

Updated the Vancouver and Ottawa location pages' related-article data in `src/data/commercialLocations.ts` from `/blog/custom-packaging-in-canada-for-multi-location-buyers-2026-08-12` to the retained September 11 route. This avoids an unnecessary 301 hop. No public URL or redirect changed.
