# Custom tuck-box buyer FAQs

Reviewed 25 November 2026. Target: `/industries/custom-tuck-boxes`. Twelve original, editorially selected buyer FAQs replace the shorter existing set. The questions reflect recurring product-page and supplier-FAQ themes; they are not a statistically measured top-twelve ranking.

## Research

- iCustomBoxes Canada: https://www.icustomboxes.ca/tuck-boxes/ — Canadian tuck-carton formats, material/print and order-planning themes.
- PackMojo folding cartons: https://packmojo.com/custom-packaging/folding-carton-boxes/ — straight/reverse tuck, snap-lock and auto-bottom construction; dimensions, samples, artwork variants and production steps.
- Packfino tuck end boxes: https://packfino.com/box-styles/tuck-end-boxes/ — US retail carton uses, fitting, flat packing, dielines and print/finish themes.
- GS1 Canada: https://www.gs1ca.org/barcodes/ and https://gs1ca.org/gs1ca-components/documents/Barcoding-for-Designers-Printers-and-Packagers.pdf — barcode dimensions, clear space, folds and edge placement.
- CFIA: https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/bilingual-food-labelling — Canadian consumer prepackaged food generally needs mandatory French/English information, with exemptions. No blanket rule applied to all product categories.
- Health Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-nutrition/food-safety/packaging-materials.html — review food packaging against the intended application; no blanket material-approval promise.
- FDA: https://www.fda.gov/food/packaging-food-contact-substances-fcs/determining-regulatory-status-components-food-contact-material — food-contact component status and conditions of use.
- FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/environmental-claims-summary-green-guides — qualify and substantiate recycled-content/recyclability claims and local access.

## Final coverage

1. Straight/reverse tuck, snap-lock and auto-bottom structures.
2. Maximum finished product dimensions, internal/external measurements and fit.
3. Paperboard grade, caliper, crease and closure performance.
4. Dense or fragile products, locking bases, inserts and outer shippers.
5. Specification-specific minimums and artwork/size variants.
6. Quotation inputs, tooling, printed area and landed cost/currency.
7. Inside/outside printing, foil, embossing, windows and fold/glue placement.
8. Dielines, barcode panels and destination-approved labels.
9. Digital, structural and printed samples, approvals and reorder references.
10. Flat delivery, pre-gluing, manual assembly and machine trials.
11. Canada/USA delivery and separate artwork, sampling, production and transit stages.
12. Application-specific food contact and finished-pack recycling evidence.
