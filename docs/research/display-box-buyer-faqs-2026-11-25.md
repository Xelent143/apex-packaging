# Display-box buyer FAQs

Reviewed 25 November 2026 for `/industries/display-boxes`. Twelve original FAQs replace the eight short existing questions. The selection reflects recurring supplier product-page themes and retail planning needs, not a statistically measured top-twelve search or enquiry ranking.

## Research reviewed

- Emenac Packaging Canada: https://www.emenacpackaging.ca/custom/display-boxes/ — countertop placement, headers, branding panels, inserts, product access and assembly themes.
- PackMojo: https://packmojo.com/custom-packaging/display-boxes/ — custom product counts/dimensions, paperboard versus corrugated options, dust flaps and perforated conversion from closed carton to display.
- Zigpac: https://www.zigpac.com/custom-boxes/custom-counter-display-boxes.html — specification-led counter/PDQ programs, footprint, capacity, board, artwork, inserts, packout and loaded prototypes.
- Walmart official supplier requirements, Packaging section: https://corporate.walmart.com/suppliers/requirements?pdf-print=1 — current specifications cover durability, dimensions, markings, labelling and pallet arrangements. The linked 69 MB guide was too large for the web reader; no detailed rule from that guide or third-party older copies was used. No claim of retailer endorsement or automatic acceptance is made.
- GS1 Canada: https://www.gs1ca.org/barcodes/ — barcode dimensions, clear space and appropriate print placement.
- FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/environmental-claims-summary-green-guides — substantiate and qualify recycled-content/recyclability claims and recycling access.

Canadian and US supplier search results were also reviewed for recurring commercial themes. Other suppliers' pricing, minimums, promises of increased sales, free samples and turnaround guarantees were not adopted. Apex-specific production and delivery statements follow existing site copy.

## Final topics

1. Countertop, PDQ and shelf-ready format selection.
2. Capacity, facings, footprint, header height and product removal.
3. Loaded and part-empty stability, materials and product retention.
4. Branding visibility, folding headers, barcodes and market artwork.
5. Retailer-specific briefs, prototype review and approval responsibilities.
6. Minimums, variants, shared structures and seasonal/store assortments.
7. RFQ inputs, setup, assembly, delivery scope and comparable total cost.
8. Loaded structural prototypes, printed samples, proofs and testing criteria.
9. Flat, assembled and preloaded formats, setup labour and responsibilities.
10. Transit protection, master cartons, headers and tear-away/perforation trials.
11. Canada/USA delivery, overseas production, approvals and separate transit time.
12. Recycled content, complete-pack recyclability and retailer disposal arrangements.
