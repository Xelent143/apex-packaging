# Mailer-box buyer FAQs

Reviewed 25 November 2026 for `/services/mailer-boxes`, as identified by the user's screenshot. Replaces the existing eight brief questions with twelve original FAQs for Canadian and US mailer buyers. The set is editorially selected from recurring supplier-FAQ themes, not a measured popularity ranking.

## Topics

1. Corrugated mailers versus shipping cartons and rigid gift boxes.
2. Complete-packout measurements, internal versus external dimensions and sample fit.
3. E/B flute and kraft/white liner choices without an unsupported universal load rating.
4. Specification-specific minimums and artwork variants.
5. Setup, print, inserts, delivered cost and currency.
6. Inside/outside print, artwork on folds, liner colour and finishes.
7. Digital, structural and printed samples and approval references.
8. Fragile items, subscription assortments, inserts and special protection.
9. Direct shipment, locks, adhesive/tear strips, outer shippers and return closures.
10. Finished outside dimensions and carrier-specific dimensional-weight rules.
11. Canada/USA delivery, Canada-based project management, overseas production and separate transit timing.
12. Complete-pack recyclability versus recycled content, local access and documentation.

## Sources

- Packwire FAQ: https://packwire.com/faq — recurring box selection, sizing, minimums, pricing, artwork, samples, production and shipping questions.
- Packlane product information: https://packlane.com/support/product-information — print surfaces, proofs, sample purpose, artwork review and separate production/transit stages.
- VistaPrint Canada mailers: https://www.vistaprint.ca/packaging/mailer-boxes/custom-mailer-boxes — Canadian buyer themes around printing, sizes and physical samples.
- UPS Canada: https://www.ups.com/ca/en/support/shipping-support/packaging-tips — suitable outer packaging, cushioning, liquid containment and closures.
- FedEx: https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packaging/what-is-dimensional-weight.html — parcel volume, actual versus dimensional weight and efficient package sizing. No universal divisor or rate promise added.
- FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/environmental-claims-summary-green-guides — distinguish and substantiate environmental claims; qualify local recycling availability.
