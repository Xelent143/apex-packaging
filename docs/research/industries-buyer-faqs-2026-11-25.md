# Industries directory: 12 buyer FAQs

Reviewed 25 November 2026. Applies to `/industries`, with the FAQ block directly after the product-category grid and an anchor link above it. These are editorially selected common buying questions, not a statistically ranked list of search volumes or customer enquiries. They cover the site's 44 product categories through shared buying questions and product-family questions.

## Research and application

- Canadian supplier FAQ: https://novapack.ca/faq/faq — question themes: quotation inputs, dimensions, material, order quantity, samples, timing and delivery. Other suppliers' minimums, pricing, lead times and refunds were not transferred to Apex.
- US supplier FAQ: https://www.customboxusa.com/faqs/ — question themes: minimums, customisation, proofs and ordering. No supplier-specific promise reused.
- Health Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-nutrition/food-safety/packaging-materials.html — food-contact safety depends on the material and intended use; do not imply all packaging is pre-approved.
- FDA: https://www.fda.gov/food/packaging-food-contact-substances-fcs/determining-regulatory-status-components-food-contact-material — review the regulatory status of food-contact components for their intended use, rather than make a blanket FDA-approved claim.
- CFIA: https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/consumers/understanding-food-labels — Canadian food labels generally require French and English, with exemptions.
- GS1: https://support.gs1.org/support/solutions/articles/43000734141-what-should-i-check-to-ensure-good-quality-barcodes — size, contrast, quiet zones and appropriate placement.
- UPS Canada: https://www.ups.com/ca/en/support/shipping-support/packaging-tips — match carton strength and cushioning to the contents; protect and immobilise items.
- Health Canada cannabis guide: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/cannabis-regulations-licensed-producers/packaging-labelling-guide-cannabis-products.html — regulated packaging needs product- and market-specific review; decorative structures are not evidence of child resistance or compliance.
- FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/environmental-claims-summary-green-guides — qualify environmental claims and substantiate recycled-content, recyclability and compostability claims appropriately.
- Competition Bureau Canada: https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/how-we-foster-competition/education-and-outreach/environmental-claims-and-greenwashing — avoid misleading environmental claims; current guidance reflects the 2026 legislative change. No outdated methodology requirement was added.

## Coverage

1. Format selection: rigid, gift, folding/tuck, pillow, window, display, retail/product, corrugated and shipping formats.
2. Quote inputs, sizing, units and comparable total price: every category.
3. MOQ, size/design combinations, launches, Christmas and holiday ranges.
4. Logos, colour, finishes, barcodes and destination-specific approved labels.
5. Digital proofs, physical samples, testing and reorder specifications.
6. Canada/USA delivery, overseas production, timing and delivery responsibilities.
7. Food, bakery, pizza, chocolate/chocolate bars, cereal, coffee, tea and spice.
8. Mylar bags and pouches, barrier, capacity, closures and application review.
9. Bottles, candles, kitchenware, electronics, auto parts and hardware: protection and suitable outer shippers.
10. Cosmetics, soap/kraft soap, apparel, hair extensions, jewellery, software kits, stationery and sports products: premium and retail packaging.
11. CBD/CBD gifts, hemp, pre-rolls, vape, cigarettes, pharmaceuticals and health products: product/market-specific specifications.
12. Complete-pack materials, recycled content, recyclability and compostability.

The visible answers and FAQPage JSON-LD use one data array. Matching structured data supports machine readability; no Google rich-result or ranking promise is made. Existing product pages, imagery and commercial policies are unchanged.
