# Services directory: 12 buyer FAQs

Reviewed 25 November 2026. The user clarified that the destination is `/services`; the mailer-box page is outside this release. The FAQs sit immediately after the six-service matrix, with a top anchor link. These are editorially selected common procurement questions, not a measured ranking by enquiry or search volume.

## Coverage

1. Choosing between corrugated, mailer/rigid, flexible, industrial, protective and design services.
2. Quote inputs, units, usable dimensions and Canada/USA receiving locations.
3. Product-specific minimums, mixed dimensions and artwork versions.
4. Setup charges, currency and comparable delivered cost.
5. Canada-based management, overseas production, production versus transit time and delivery scope.
6. Structural design, dielines, print, finishes and artwork responsibilities.
7. Digital proofs, structural/printed samples, trials and reorder references.
8. Polybags, pouches, wicket/zipper/slider formats and paper-bag carrying requirements.
9. Bulk containers, liners, stretch versus shrink film and pallet/load trials.
10. Inserts, foam, void fill, static-control and corrosion protection.
11. Application-specific food-contact, medical and pharmaceutical requirements; no blanket compliance or sterility promise.
12. Recycled content, recyclability, compostability and complete-pack evidence.

## Sources reviewed

- Nova Packaging (Canada): https://novapack.ca/faq/faq — RFQ fields, material/structure, quantities, timeline, samples and delivered-order questions.
- Packwire: https://packwire.com/faq — recurring questions about box choice, minimums/samples, pricing, artwork, production, shipping and pallet receiving.
- Packlane: https://packlane.com/support/product-information — proofing, artwork, inside/outside print, samples and production versus transit timing.
- VistaPrint Canada: https://www.vistaprint.ca/packaging/mailer-boxes/custom-mailer-boxes — artwork and sample question themes. Its specifications and commercial policies were not adopted.
- UPS Canada: https://www.ups.com/ca/en/support/shipping-support/packaging-tips — suitable outer packaging, cushioning, liquid containment and electronics precautions.
- FedEx: https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packaging/what-is-dimensional-weight.html — reviewed for shipping-cost context; no universal carrier rate or divisor added.
- Health Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-nutrition/food-safety/packaging-materials.html — suitability of food-contact materials for their intended use; letters of no objection do not constitute blanket approvals.
- FDA: https://www.fda.gov/food/packaging-food-contact-substances-fcs/determining-regulatory-status-components-food-contact-material — component status and intended conditions of use.
- FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/environmental-claims-summary-green-guides — substantiate and qualify environmental claims; distinguish recycled content, recyclability and compostability.
